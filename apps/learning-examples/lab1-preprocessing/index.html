See what happens to the numbers

Two small examples from the lab questions: filling a gap and changing the scale of a reading.

1. What replaces NaN?

NaN marks a missing reading. Linear interpolation divides the change between two known readings into equal steps when samples are equally spaced.

This assumes equal sample spacing, as in the lab. It estimates missing values; it does not recover the original measurements.

2. Why subtract the mean and divide by standard deviation?

The mean sets the centre. The standard deviation measures spread and sets the size of one step on the new scale. The result says how many standard deviations a reading is above or below the training mean.

Changing the current to transform does not change the training mean or standard deviation. A test reading can be outside the training range.
